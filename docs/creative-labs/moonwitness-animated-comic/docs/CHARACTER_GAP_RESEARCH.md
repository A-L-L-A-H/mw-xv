## 3. Synthesis: Why `EP-LAB-01` Uses `kuro-signal`, `vesper-ink`, and Non-Canonical `PCC-SIGNAL-SWITCHBOARD-01`

1. **Zero Unauthorized Canon Promotion:** Because every `CHR-0001`..`CHR-0019` entry is either `lore / review` (MW-II) or a separate generation's working cast with unresolved cross-generation links and strict theological/copyright guardrails (MW-XII), none of them can be silently dropped into an MW-XV animated comic as a newly drawn face.
2. **100% Approved Visual Continuity On-Screen:** `kuro-signal` (Signal Courier) and `vesper-ink` (Zine Archivist) are already approved in `packages/assets/source/world/characters/character-registry.json` with locked palettes, props, and master references.
3. **Safe Experimental Expansion:** To test how a third supporting role could bridge rooftop signal reception and archive preservation without colliding with `CHR-0002` (`A LAH`), `CHR-0013` (`4-LAH / THE BATTERIES`), or `CHR-0017` (`RECORD TWINS`), we propose **`PCC-SIGNAL-SWITCHBOARD-01`** in [`docs/PROPOSED_CHARACTER_CANDIDATE.md`](file:///z:/repo/mw-xv/docs/creative-labs/moonwitness-animated-comic/docs/PROPOSED_CHARACTER_CANDIDATE.md) under an explicit non-canonical `PCC-*` namespace with `humanReviewRequired: true`.
