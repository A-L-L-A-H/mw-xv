# Consumer Experience Visual Audit (`apps/board` & `apps/ui-catalog`)

> **Scope & Method**: Live Chromium visual inspection via Playwright (`@playwright/test@1.63.0`) against running local servers (`@moonwitness/api` `visual-audit:server` on `127.0.0.1:3027`, `@moonwitness/board` Next.js 16.4.6 on `127.0.0.1:5187`, `@moonwitness/ui-catalog` Vite preview on `127.0.0.1:4184/moonwitness-xiv/components/`). Audited across 4 viewports (`1440x900` desktop, `1280x800` laptop, `768x1024` tablet, `390x844` mobile), light & dark modes, all 3 built-in skins (`moonwitness-ink`, `moonwitness-compact`, `moonwitness-high-contrast`), `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, and interactive states (command palette, shortcuts dialog, mobile nav sheets, auth loading/error, empty search, SceneLab, FXLab, AssetBrowser).

## 1. Executive Visual Summary

MoonWitness exhibits a sharp **two-tier visual split**:

1. **High-Impact Editorial Entry Surfaces** ([`/`](../../../apps/board/src/app/page.tsx), [`/login`](../../../apps/board/src/app/login/page.tsx), [`/register`](../../../apps/board/src/app/register/page.tsx), [`/board` masthead](../../../apps/board/src/board-pages/dashboard-page.tsx)) showcase strong manga/zine character, brush display typography (`MoonWitness Grunge`), halftone paper grain, and Moon City hero illustrations.

## 3. Deep-Dive Visual Defect Analysis

### 3.1 Stretched `--mw-chalk-mask` Collision Defect (P0)

- **Root Cause**: [`packages/ui/src/styles/tokens.css#L243`](../../../packages/ui/src/styles/tokens.css#L243) defines `--mw-chalk-mask` as a `viewBox="0 0 200 100" preserveAspectRatio="none"` SVG with thick `stroke-width="4.2"` outer dashes plus interior strokes (`M4 6L52 4 ...`) and corner circles (`r="2"`). [`packages/ui/src/styles/components.css#L1049-L1075`](../../../packages/ui/src/styles/components.css#L1049-L1075), [`apps/board/src/index.css#L157-L173`](../../../apps/board/src/index.css#L157-L173), and [`apps/board/src/app/global.css#L414-L431`](../../../apps/board/src/app/global.css#L414-L431) apply this mask at `mask-size: 100% 100%` over `inset: 0; z-index: 2..3`.
- **Observed Failure**: Whenever a panel is tall (mobile partner card in [audit-partner-list-mobile.png](./screenshots/audit-partner-list-mobile.png)) or wide (data table in [audit-partner-list-desktop.png](./screenshots/audit-partner-list-desktop.png)), non-uniform scaling blows the 2–4px strokes up into **20–45px solid dark/white blocks** directly over interactive text, badges, table column headers, and close buttons ([audit-board-command-palette.png](./screenshots/audit-board-command-palette.png), [audit-board-shortcuts-dialog.png](./screenshots/audit-board-shortcuts-dialog.png)).
- **Fix Direction**: Replace stretched `mask-size: 100% 100%` overlays with CSS `border-image` 9-slice SVG (fixed corner/edge thickness) or `border-radius: var(--mw-grunge-radius-hand)` + crisp 2px borders and discrete corner ink marks outside content padding (`--mw-torn-safe-inset`).

### 3.2 Dark Mode Contrast Collapse & Unstable Remounts on Settings (P0)

- **Root Cause 1 (Contrast)**: In [`apps/board/src/board-pages/settings-page.tsx#L830-L920`](../../../apps/board/src/board-pages/settings-page.tsx#L830-L920), unselected Theme, Skin, and Density cards render as `<Button>` without `variant="outline"`, inheriting `.mw-ui-button`'s bright yellow `--mw-primary` (`#ffe031`) background and `--mw-on-accent` (`#0d0d0d`) foreground. Inside those buttons, skin descriptions use `text-ink-soft` (`--mw-ink-soft`). In `.dark` ([`tokens.css#L275`](../../../packages/ui/src/styles/tokens.css#L275)), `--mw-ink-soft` becomes `#c9c5b8` (warm cream), yielding **~1.3:1 contrast** against `#ffe031` yellow—making descriptions inside `MOONWITNESS COMPACT` and `MOONWITNESS HIGH CONTRAST` nearly invisible in [audit-settings-ink-dark.png](./screenshots/audit-settings-ink-dark.png).
- **Root Cause 2 (DOM Detachment)**: During Playwright interaction on `/board/settings`, standard click targeting detached repeatedly (`element was detached from the DOM`) because [`SettingsPage`](../../../apps/board/src/board-pages/settings-page.tsx#L175-L180) keys `<SettingsPageContent key={scopeKey} />` on mutable `getQueryScope()` and background queries (`/auth/me`, `/notifications/inbox`) remain active, causing frequent re-renders/skeleton persistence ([audit-settings-light-desktop.png](./screenshots/audit-settings-light-desktop.png), [audit-profile-desktop.png](./screenshots/audit-profile-desktop.png)).

### 3.3 Responsive Clipping & Route Accessibility (P1)

1. **Mobile Home Hero (`390x844`)**: `.home-title` sets `white-space: nowrap` ([`global.css#L255`](../../../apps/board/src/app/global.css#L255)) inside `.home-hero { overflow: hidden }`, clipping the final `"S"` of `MOONWITNESS` ([audit-home-mobile.png](./screenshots/audit-home-mobile.png)).
2. **Tablet Topbar (`768x1024`)**: `#open-command-palette` in [`app-shell.tsx#L527-L538`](../../../apps/board/src/components/layout/app-shell.tsx#L527-L538) shrinks to `~48px` wide between the mobile menu button and 7 action buttons, showing only `"CTRL"` ([audit-board-dashboard-tablet.png](./screenshots/audit-board-dashboard-tablet.png)).
3. **Mobile Nav Sheet Close Collision**: `.board-sidebar::after` ([`index.css#L229-L247`](../../../apps/board/src/index.css#L229-L247)) overlaps the `SheetContent` close `X` button on mobile ([audit-board-mobile-nav-sheet.png](./screenshots/audit-board-mobile-nav-sheet.png)).
4. **Unreachable `/knowledge` Shell**: [`next.config.ts#L54`](../../../apps/board/next.config.ts#L54) permanently redirects `/knowledge` to `/board/knowledge`, routing unauthenticated users to `/login?returnTo=%2Fboard%2Fknowledge` ([audit-knowledge-desktop.png](./screenshots/audit-knowledge-desktop.png)) and leaving [`apps/board/src/app/knowledge/page.tsx`](../../../apps/board/src/app/knowledge/page.tsx) unreachable.
5. **UI Catalog Mobile Fixed Height & Typos**: [`apps/ui-catalog/src/styles.css#L1418-L1425`](../../../apps/ui-catalog/src/styles.css#L1418-L1425) hardcodes `height: 17.5rem; overflow: hidden` on `.mw-ui-page-header` below `42rem`; [`AssetBrowser.tsx#L40-L45`](../../../apps/ui-catalog/src/AssetBrowser.tsx#L40-L45) renders `"All categorys"` and `"All statuss"` ([audit-catalog-assets-desktop.png](./screenshots/audit-catalog-assets-desktop.png)).

### 3.4 Flat SaaS Operational Views vs. Comic/Character Identity

While `/` (`audit-home-desktop.png`), `/login` (`audit-login-desktop.png`), and `/board` masthead (`audit-board-dashboard-desktop.png`) deliver a cohesive comic-zine world, operational pages revert to **generic admin SaaS templates**:

- **`/board/ai/hq`** ([audit-ai-hq-desktop.png](./screenshots/audit-ai-hq-desktop.png)): Full-width stacked native `<select>` bars (`All Role Templates`, `All Lifecycle States`), dense rectangular boxes, and zero character portraits or agent avatars for service-principal agents (`Example Bounded Researcher`, `Demo Workspace Builder`).
- **`/board/studio`** ([audit-studio-desktop.png](./screenshots/audit-studio-desktop.png)): Plain white-box form + 4 flat text tabs; despite being the Creative & Editorial Studio for _GOD PROMISE & ROCKSOUL_, it contains zero visual artwork previews, zero imprint badges/stamps, and mixes Indonesian empty-state copy ([`editorial-studio-panel.tsx#L395-L406`](../../../apps/board/src/components/creative/editorial-studio-panel.tsx#L395-L406)) into an English screen.
- **Zero Character/Scene Runtime Integration in `apps/board`**: [`CharacterPreview`](../../../packages/ui/src/components/character-preview.tsx), [`LayeredScene`](../../../packages/ui/src/components/layered-scene.tsx), [`SpritePlayer`](../../../packages/ui/src/components/sprite-player.tsx), [`SceneParticles`](../../../packages/ui/src/components/scene-particles.tsx), and [`ComicSceneTransition`](../../../packages/ui/src/components/scene-transitions.tsx) exist in `@moonwitness/ui` and render in SceneLab ([audit-catalog-scenelab-desktop.png](./screenshots/audit-catalog-scenelab-desktop.png)), but **FXLab** ([audit-catalog-fxlab-desktop.png](./screenshots/audit-catalog-fxlab-desktop.png)) confirms `SceneParticles` and `ComicSceneTransition` have no CSS rules in [`components.css`](../../../packages/ui/src/styles/components.css) and render blank containers.

### 3.5 Unillustrated Empty, Error & Progression/Sticker Gaps

- **Empty & Error States**: [`EmptyState`](../../../packages/ui/src/components/empty-state.tsx) supports only a small `icon` slot and text. [`ListView`](../../../apps/board/src/components/views/list-view.tsx#L794-L803) renders a `32px` `<Doodle kind="sparkle" />` ([audit-partner-list-empty.png](./screenshots/audit-partner-list-empty.png)), [`DashboardPage`](../../../apps/board/src/board-pages/dashboard-page.tsx) renders plain text `"No recent audit records available."` ([audit-board-dashboard-desktop.png](./screenshots/audit-board-dashboard-desktop.png)), and [`KanbanView`](../../../apps/board/src/components/views/kanban-view.tsx#L61-L65) / [`FormView`](../../../apps/board/src/components/views/form-view.tsx#L65-L90) / [`SettingsPage`](../../../apps/board/src/board-pages/settings-page.tsx#L162-L171) / [`ProfilePage`](../../../apps/board/src/board-pages/profile-page.tsx#L115-L127) use plain unillustrated boxes.
- **Progression & Sticker Grammar**: Only a single static footer pill (`+1 EVERY DAY` in [`app-shell.tsx#L442-L444`](../../../apps/board/src/components/layout/app-shell.tsx#L442-L444)) exists. There are no `StickerBadge`, `QuestCard`, `AchievementBadge`, or `RewardPanel` primitives for onboarding checklists, publication readiness gates, or daily activity streaks.
- **Typography Shadowing & CSP Console Warnings**: `audit-metrics.json` confirms `MoonWitness Grunge` is loaded first in both `--mw-font-brush` and `--mw-font-display` on Board ([`tokens.css#L81-L84`](../../../packages/ui/src/styles/tokens.css#L81-L84)), shadowing `Road Rage` and `Anton` (whereas UI Catalog overrides `--catalog-font-display` to `'Anton'` first). Next.js dev mode logs 19 CSP `eval()` console errors due to [`next.config.ts#L40`](../../../apps/board/next.config.ts#L40) omitting `'unsafe-eval'` in dev.
