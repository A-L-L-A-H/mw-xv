## 1. Non-negotiable separation

Every future record must distinguish:
1. Arabic/source-language scripture text.
2. Textual witness, work, edition and passage.
3. Translation / transliteration.
4. Tafsir or scholarly commentary, with author/tradition/edition.
5. Historical context and independent research.
6. Owner's interpretation or personal reflection.
7. Metaphor, framework and creative narrative.
8. AI-generated hypothesis or summary.

The target's existing ontology already distinguishes `RevelationIdentity`, `TextualWitness`, `Canon` and `Edition`. A relation or list membership does not prove identity equivalence or clear source rights.

## 7. Existing research-domain split

Selected source repositories describe a provenance-first research separation:
- TEXT / RGBL: exact source text, editions, passages, rights, variants and provenance.
- STORY / MFTL: narratives and belief claims.
- EVENT / LEGEND: historical event reconstruction.
- PERSON / SUPERHERO: actors, witness, authorship and transmission.
- LAW / AWS: authority, jurisdiction and legal applicability.
- PERSPECTIVE / JIZZ: how a phenomenon is framed and viewed.
- RELATIONSHIP / CORRELATION: typed cross-domain links with uncertainty.

This is a repository-documented domain model, not proof that the whole research system is production-certified or that every external source may be copied.

## 8. Publication and safety gates

- Verify scripture and hadith quotations against exact source editions; do not fabricate wording or scholarly consensus.
- Keep translation and commentary attribution visible.
- A personal reflection must not be presented as scripture or tafsir.
- A pattern or number sequence is not proof of prophecy, intent or supernatural causation.
- No project slogan gives divine authorization or guarantees outcomes.
- Private testimony is not automatically public corpus content.
- No private or child-sensitive archive content is sent to general search, embeddings, analytics or hosted inference.
- No public release until source rights, consent, provenance and human review are recorded.
