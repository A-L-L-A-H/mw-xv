- Product name: **MoonWitness**.
- Indonesian gloss in Issue #16: **BULAN BERSAKSI**.

## 8. Brand governance

- Keep project name, repository name, domain name, acronym and product title in separate fields.
- Preserve exact original slogan; store polished text as a proposed editorial variant.
- Do not imply a user-owned brand has legal trademark clearance.
- Do not expose credentials, private endpoints, internal networks, customer names, IDs or operational logs.
- Every image/logotype/font/sound needs creator/source, license/permission, revision and publication status before being promoted as canonical asset.
