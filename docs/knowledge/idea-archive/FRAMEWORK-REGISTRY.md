## FRM-0001 — OMNISCIENCE METHOD

The source paper defines four epistemic operations:

| Layer | Question | Output | Stage value |
|---|---|---|---:|
| R — Reality | What actually happened? | Reality Set: observations, records, claims and source evidence | 1 |
| G — Guidance | What should guide interpretation? | Guidance Set: sourced/versioned principles, rules, texts and values | 2 |
| B — Balance | How should facts and guidance be weighed in context? | Balanced Assessment: competing evidence, uncertainty, alternatives and consequences | 3 |
| L — Light | What becomes clear after the preceding steps? | Traceable synthesis / insight | 4 |

The four stage numbers are **ordinal stage identifiers**, not probability or confidence. The paper says confidence is stored separately.

Operational requirements specified by the source:
- Minimum provenance chain: Light claim → Balance record → Reality items + Guidance items.
- Reported / uncertain / disputed facts must not silently become verified.
- Guidance source, version and scope remain explicit.
- Balance records context, conflict, counter-evidence, bias checks and alternative interpretations.
- Light includes assumptions, unresolved questions and provenance.
- Human review remains required; AI output is not divine revelation, absolute truth or infallible authority.

### Qur'anic-reference boundary

The paper uses Al-Hujurat 49:6, 49:9–10, 49:11–12 and 49:13 as **conceptual anchors** for verification, justice/settlement, conduct and mutual knowing. It explicitly states that R–G–B–L is the author's interpretive construction, not a formal method named by the Qur'an. Exact verse and translation editions need source review before publication.

The paper retains `Breath Carolina` as author-defined terminology and explicitly does not present it as an established scientific term. This public registry records the method and its stated epistemic limits; it does not reproduce personal first-person experiential notes from the Library source.

## FRM-0015 — CRAYON Framework v1.3

**Source:** `CRAYON_Framework_v1.3_Bilingual_FINAL.docx`, Library, 23 August 2026; [Issue #173](https://github.com/A-L-L-A-H/mw-xv/issues/173). A separate companion paper, `Easy_Way_to_Understand_Allah_Bilingual_Research_Paper_v2_FINAL.docx`, uses the framework as a non-scriptural conceptual starting point.

- Four cumulative levels: `R1 = Reality`; `G2 = R1 + Governing Order`; `B3 = R1 + G2 + Being`; `L4 = R1 + G2 + B3 + Integration/Insight`.
- Clock metaphor: 12:00 R1, 3:00 G2, 6:00 B3, 9:00 L4. The clock is a teaching metaphor, not an evidence system, scientific measure or religious symbol.
- CRAYON v1.3 9 Gate: SEE, HEAR, TOUCH, TASTE, SMELL, ATTEND, REMEMBER, REASON, CHOOSE. These are information-processing categories, not a claim that humans have only nine biological senses.
- CRAYON v1.3 13 Way: OBSERVE, NOTICE, QUESTION, MEASURE, COMPARE, EXPLORE, UNDERSTAND, CONNECT, EVALUATE, REASON, CONCLUDE, ACT, LIVE.
- The paper also gives four situational audience conditions (KNOWLEDGE, RELEVANCE, CONFIDENCE, TRUST / RESISTANCE), quality checks and an iterative feedback loop.
- Status: proposed conceptual and educational framework; not a validated scientific theory or experimentally validated cognitive taxonomy. Mathematics, science and logic are tools, not standalone proof or direct empirical measurement of Allah.

**Companion-paper variation:** “An Easy Way to Understand Allah” v2 uses a different Gate wording and includes REVIEW in its 13-Way list. Do not rewrite either source to make them match. Treat the difference as an open version/application question.

## FRM-0016 — Dīn Allah / Universal Iman Foundation v1.8 file

The Library file named `Din_Allah_4_Kitab_Universal_Iman_Foundation_v1.8_FINAL_Universal_Iman_Charter.docx` ([Issue #174](https://github.com/A-L-L-A-H/mw-xv/issues/174)) is a four-book, Qur’an-governed comparative research framework. The source distinguishes Qur’anic concepts from the author’s analytical synthesis. It describes Taurat, Zabur, Injil and Qur’an as four source families, with Qur’an as a supervisory criterion in its own research model. It explicitly cautions that current textual witnesses must not be assumed identical to the original revelations named by the Qur’an. It uses source classes such as Q-EXPLICIT, TRADITION, SYNTHESIS and HYPOTHESIS. Its proposed evidence score is an analysis heuristic, not a measure of divine truth. This is a research paper, not a new religion, revealed creed or scholarly consensus claim.

## No silent framework merger

- RGBL epistemic method is not the same as RGB colour / Memory–Ruhani–Nature poster imagery.
- CRAYON `R1 / G2 / B3 / L4` is not the same as OMNISCIENCE RGBL `Reality / Guidance / Balance / Light`; their G and B definitions differ.
- Mizan is not automatically the same as the OM Balance stage, 9 Gates, 13 Ways or Q4Q.
- HISTA is a distinct acronym and does not prove that it is the Four Pillars model.
- 1·6·3 and 72→73 remain conceptual seeds, not accepted religious doctrine.
- A number/pattern relationship is not evidence of prophecy, authorial intent or supernatural causation.
