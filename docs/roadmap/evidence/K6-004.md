- Added `packages/research/src/child-protection-gate.schema.json` (JSON Schema 2020-12) and `validateChildProtectionGateInput` / `evaluateChildProtectionAndPublicationGate` / `containsProhibitedMinorIdentifiers` in `packages/research/src/child-protection-gate.ts`.
- Enforced upward classification inheritance without downgrade (`PUBLIC_APPROVED < INTERNAL < CONFIDENTIAL < RESTRICTED < CHILD_SENSITIVE`), forcing `CHILD_SENSITIVE` whenever `minor_involved === true` or `program_slot === 13`.
- Enforced default-deny on `public_projection` and `public_url` unless `effective_classification === "PUBLIC_APPROVED"`, zero minor identifiers are detected, and a separate minimized `publication_approval` (`reviewer_id !== requester_id`, matching SHA-256 digests, non-empty `decision_ref`) is verified.
- Blocked `CHILD_SENSITIVE` / `minor_involved` records unconditionally on `public_projection`, `public_url`, `search_index`, `ai_prompt`, `analytics`, and `log_output`, and blocked internal/escalation access unless `safeguarding_process_approved === true`, `synthetic === true`, zero minor PII paths are detected, and an independent `escalation_approval` is present.
- Added `docs/governance/child-protection-and-publication-risk-gates.md` documenting disclosure protection, minor-PII prohibitions, and human-reviewed escalation gates.
- Added `apps/api/src/routes/child-protection.routes.ts` (`POST /api/research/child-protection/gate-check`) enforcing authentication, `superadmin` + active company membership, `Cache-Control: private, no-store`, and sanitized hash-only responses without echoing raw candidate payloads or minor identifiers.
- Added 5 Vitest tests in `packages/research/tests/child-protection-gate.test.ts` and 3 Fastify API route tests in `apps/api/tests/child-protection.routes.test.ts`.
