# Evidence K6-002 — scoped analytic responsibility engine (optional)

## Status

- Status: in progress; the disabled-by-default scoped analytic responsibility contract, JSON Schema, and synthetic golden/missing-data/override/fail-closed tests are implemented in `@moonwitness/research`. Activation remains gated on owner/CODEOWNER approval of [K6-001 / ADR 0005](../../decisions/0005-mizan-evaluation-policy-boundary.md) and a reviewed policy profile.
- Date: 2026-11-21 (Asia/Jakarta).
- Dependencies: K6-001 (`approval-required` open), K3-002 (complete).
- Scope: `ontology/domains/analytic-responsibility.schema.json`, `packages/research/src/analytic-responsibility.ts`, `packages/research/src/index.ts`, and `packages/research/tests/analytic-responsibility.test.ts`.

## Implemented

- Added `ontology/domains/analytic-responsibility.schema.json` (JSON Schema 2020-12) defining `schema_version: 1`, `policy_profile_id`, `policy_version`, explicit `scope`, factor weights summing to `1.0`, `evidence_ids`, and optional `human_override`.
- Added `validateAnalyticResponsibilityRequest` and `evaluateScopedAnalyticResponsibility` in `packages/research/src/analytic-responsibility.ts`.
- Enforced disabled-by-default execution (`status: "disabled"`, `reason: "analytic_engine_disabled_by_default"`) unless `{ enabled: true }` is explicitly passed.
- Enforced `K3-002` evidence binding and `ADR 0005` policy boundaries: measured factors require valid, accepted, readable canonical evidence; missing, conflicting, unreviewed, or denied evidence degrades to `unknown` (`numeric_aggregate: null`, never coerced to zero); every response carries `normative_boundary` with `advisory_only: true` and `false` for `normative_verdicts_allowed`, `certifies_human_worth`, `certifies_salvation`, `certifies_divine_judgment`, `certifies_legal_status`, and `autonomous_action_allowed`.
- Added 5 Vitest test suites in `packages/research/tests/analytic-responsibility.test.ts` covering disabled-by-default behavior, reproducible golden factor calculation with pinned `output_digest`, missing/unreviewed/denied evidence staying `unknown`, explicit human overrides preserving computed factors, and fail-closed rejection of normative fields, invalid weights, missing evidence IDs, and async mutation.
