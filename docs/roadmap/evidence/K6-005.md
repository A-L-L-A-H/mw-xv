## Implemented

- Added `verifyBugCaseOutcomeWithEvidence` in `packages/orm-knowledge/src/bug-outcome-verification.ts`:
  - Validates submitted evidence against `K3-002` (`validateResearchEvidenceReference`).
  - Resolves canonical evidence through a caller-supplied trusted resolver and fails closed on missing evidence (`MISSING_EVIDENCE`), unreadable access, non-`accepted` review status (`UNREVIEWED_EVIDENCE`), or selector/content-SHA-256 mismatch (`TAMPERED_EVIDENCE`).
  - Enforces evidence freshness (`capturedAt` ISO-8601 UTC check within `maxEvidenceAgeHours`, defaulting to `720h`) and rejects stale or future-dated timestamps (`STALE_EVIDENCE`).
  - Requires a passing `outcomeTestProof` (`passed: true`, non-empty `test_run_id` and `summary`, 64-hex `artifact_sha256`).
  - Transitions the BUG case via `transitionBugCaseWorkflow` (`action: 'verify_outcome'`), enforcing independent reviewer separation (distinct from starter and action actor), workflow state preconditions (`action_recorded -> verified_resolved`), and ensuring editorial/story publication cannot close an unresolved BUG.
- Added `reopenBugCaseOnEvidenceChange` in `packages/orm-knowledge/src/bug-outcome-verification.ts`, re-evaluating the latest verification event's evidence digest, review status, and freshness and transitioning `verified_resolved -> reopened` with an immutable `reopen` event when evidence becomes missing, unreviewed, tampered, or stale.
- Added 2 Vitest test suites in `packages/orm-knowledge/tests/bug-outcome-verification.test.ts` covering positive verification + digest-bound reopen, story-submission non-closure, missing/unreviewed/tampered/stale evidence failures, failing test proofs, and self-verification denial.
