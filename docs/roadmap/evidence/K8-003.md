## Implemented

- Added `PublicationSnapshot` (`knowledge.publication_snapshot`) in `packages/orm-knowledge/src/publication.model.ts` with append-only ORM hooks, SQLite/PostgreSQL triggers, and deny-by-default `ModelAccess` for `base.group_user` in `manifest.ts`.
- Added `recordPublicationSnapshot`, `readApprovedPublication`, and `listApprovedPublications` in `packages/orm-knowledge/src/publication-store.ts`:
  - Approving a `PUBLIC_APPROVED` snapshot enforces `rightsState === 'permitted'`, `minorInvolved === false`, independent reviewer (`requesterUserId !== actor.userId`), non-empty citations with 64-hex SHA-256 digests and `rights_evidence_ref`, and `containsProhibitedMinorIdentifiers(...) === []`.
  - Public reads inspect ONLY the latest revision per `publication_id` and fail closed (`null`) on any non-`approved`, non-`PUBLIC_APPROVED`, non-`permitted` rights, `minor_involved === true`, self-reviewed, minor-PII-containing, or digest-mismatched row.
- Added `apps/api/src/routes/publication.routes.ts` (`GET /api/public/publications` and `GET /api/public/publications/:publicationId`) setting `Cache-Control: no-store, must-revalidate` and `ETag`, returning identical `404` `{ success: false, error: 'Publication not found' }` for missing, draft, internal, restricted, or unknown-rights IDs.
- Mounted `childProtectionRoutes` and `publicationRoutes` in `apps/api/src/app.ts`.
- Added 2 Vitest suites in `packages/orm-knowledge/tests/publication-store.test.ts` and 1 API route suite in `apps/api/tests/publication.routes.test.ts`.
