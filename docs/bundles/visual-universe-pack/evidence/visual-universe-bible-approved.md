# Approved MoonWitness XV visual direction

**Approved by the user on 2026-11-22.** This is the canonical direction for new visual-universe production work. It extends the existing MoonWitness brand and UI Bible; it does not replace their token or licensing sources.

## Identity, palette, and type

Use the existing semantic palette from `packages/ui/TOKENS.md`: ink/black and warm paper/white as the foundation, hot magenta and electric cyan as restrained editorial/cinematic accents, signal yellow for high-attention callouts. Semantic success/warning/error states remain distinct, with tested contrast pairings. Use existing licensed type assets: Anton for short display, Space Grotesk for body/UI, JetBrains Mono for metadata, and Road Rage only as decorative lettering where licensed. Keep interface copy as live accessible text.

## Visual language

Create original manga/zine-inspired artwork with confident ink contour, restrained cel shading, controlled photocopy grain and halftone, occasional torn-paper edges, and hand-drawn motion marks. Do not reproduce copyrighted characters, franchise silhouettes, costumes, marks, panel layouts, or signature poses. Do not depict Allah or sacred figures. Never identify real crew/users or invent factual stories, places, metrics, or signals.

## Experience tiers

- **Product chrome:** clean, stable, readable, and interaction-first. Keep data, forms, labels, status, and controls unobscured.
- **Editorial:** restrained texture and hand-marked accents support short headings and illustrative panels.
- **Cinematic:** original layered urban night environment and brief transitions belong to immersive story surfaces, not operational tables.
- **Ambient:** sparse, low-contrast atmosphere with a complete static frame; pause offscreen and honor reduced motion.

## Moon City homepage composition

Build a layered night sky, moon, clouds, distant city, near city, and foreground. Use a bounded camera/parallax range and intentional desktop/mobile focal crops with readable copy-safe space. Place the character only after the separate pilot identity approval. Build reduced-motion/static content as a complete experience and keep scene audio off unless explicitly enabled after interaction.

## Rendering, motion, and production boundaries

Keep UI labels and dynamic content outside baked artwork. Declare source/master/generated provenance in the existing asset manifest and license records. Reuse `packages/assets`, `packages/ui`, and `apps/ui-catalog`. The approved animation boundary is CSS/SVG-first, with the existing Motion package retained for now and reconsidered only against a measured requirement. Do not add specialist or paid runtime engines without new explicit approval and proof of license, bundle, and performance.

## Approval states

This art direction is approved. The pilot character identity and final hero composition remain separate approval gates; this approval does not approve a particular character or final hero artwork.
