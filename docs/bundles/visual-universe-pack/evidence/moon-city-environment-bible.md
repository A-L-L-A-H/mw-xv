# Moon City environment kit bible and shot breakdown

## Canonical environment: Moon City night

A fictional, non-geographic urban skyline at night under an oversized moon. The scene conveys a quiet watchful atmosphere through silhouette and light, not through live signals, maps, or factual city details. Apply the approved ink-and-paper foundation with cool cyan ambient night light and rare magenta/signal-yellow focal accents. No logos, readable signage, UI copy, metrics, or real place identifiers are part of the artwork.

## Reusable depth kit

1. **Sky base:** opaque/deep ink blue field with sparse original stars; static source.
2. **Moon:** independent disc/texture with restrained edge glow; separate from sky and skyline.
3. **Cloud/haze:** transparent broad forms; either static or slow, seamless horizontal loop.
4. **Distant skyline:** low-contrast silhouettes, smallest structures, cool atmospheric falloff.
5. **Midground city:** larger rooflines and selected lit windows; no meaningful patterns that imply data.
6. **Near structures:** rooftop volumes and antennas; distinct silhouettes with alpha where needed.
7. **Foreground/props:** railing, roof edge, wires and small unlettered props; keep below text/control safe zones.
8. **Optional approved character plane:** independently addressable; never use a pilot identity before approval.

All layers share an artboard coordinate system, declared dimensions, transparent bounds and safe margins. Reuse these modules only in immersive/editorial contexts where product meaning is clear; do not copy a whole background into each addon.

## Shot breakdown: first homepage scene

- Wide view: moon upper-right, skyline horizon in lower third, left 38% quiet for live heading and controls.
- Tall view: moon above skyline, vertically stacked cloud bands and foreground reduced to a lower silhouette; choose a live-copy rail after the actual homepage layout is composed.
- Depth: sky 0; moon 0.02; clouds 0.04; distant skyline 0.08; midground 0.12; near structures 0.16; foreground 0.20. Camera movement must stay within authored overscan.
- Static fallback: one legible composite with the same visual focal point; no motion or audio required.

## Asset review

For every eventual source and derivative, register canonical manifest path, source relation, profile, dimensions, alpha, focal point, license, generated date, and approval status. Review transparency, crop-safe focal placement, contrast against live copy, and the layer contact sheet before marking production assets done. This document is a production brief; it does not assert those assets exist.
