# Moon City asset tool and review status

An image-generation tool was available through Magpie and reported model `workbuddy-ai/hy-image-3.5`; source outputs are present and inventoried. Earlier statements claiming the local image-authoring tool was unavailable were incorrect and have been superseded.

The generated master and opaque skyline source were visually inspected and used to derive a moon layer and a skyline cutout. Magpie/provider/model licensing terms have not been verified, so all AI-generated originals and derivatives are recorded as `unverified`, `blocked`, and non-distributable in `packages/assets/licenses.json`.

The current contact review (`moon-city-layer-contact-review.png`) confirms a usable broad night-city composition and separated layers, but is not a final accepted responsive scene preview: cloud forms and foreground occlusion still need refinement, and no pilot character, loopable animation, complete transparent sky set, or reviewed mobile crop exists. W4 tasks remain blocked only where acceptance criteria or dependencies are unmet; source assets are not represented as production-complete.
