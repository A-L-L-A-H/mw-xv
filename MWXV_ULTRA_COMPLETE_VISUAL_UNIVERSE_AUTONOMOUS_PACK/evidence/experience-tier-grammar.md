# Experience-tier grammar

| Tier | Surfaces | Decoration and movement | Acceptance boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Product chrome | Board shell, navigation, forms, record lists and operational controls | Stable paper/ink; restrained focus/press feedback; no persistent scene or grain under dense data | Readability, permissions, keyboard, contrast and visible state always lead |
| Editorial | Landing-page modules, short headings, curated explanatory panels | Controlled manga/zine framing, light halftone, ink marks and short transitions | Keep all labels and copy as live text; decoration cannot encode data/status |
| Cinematic | Approved Living World scenes and story environments | Layered original illustration, bounded parallax and scene-local ambient movement | Complete static frame; pause offscreen; support reduced motion and responsive crops |
| Ambient | Decorative backdrop in an approved immersive scene | Slow, sparse clouds/stars/grain, low contrast and no perpetual high-frequency movement | Decorative only, no focus or pointer capture; offscreen pause; reduced-motion static state |

Animation categories are distinct: micro feedback is brief and action/state-linked; editorial motion emphasizes a heading/panel once; cinematic motion coordinates scene composition; ambient motion is sparse atmosphere. Motion never supplies essential meaning, blocks navigation, changes record semantics, or plays sound without opt-in. Respect the approved UI and Motion boundaries.

**Examples:** operational partner tables remain clean data surfaces; a landing editorial card may use one manga frame and ink accent; a future approved Moon City hero may contain separated city layers and short transition; its static still and interface copy remain complete when motion is disabled.
